# Pattern entry — body template

Use this as the `--body-file` when adding a pattern. The frontmatter is written for you by
`kb.py add`; this is the part you fill in.

Keep it short. A pattern entry that takes ten minutes to read is a document, not a library
entry — and it will not be read on the day it matters.

---

## Problem

The user or business problem this solves. Be concrete about who was stuck and on what.

## Mechanism

What is structurally happening — layout, timing, state, interaction. Enough that you could
rebuild it from this paragraph alone, without the original reference in front of you.

## Why it works

The perceptual, cognitive, or behavioural reason. Not "it looks clean". If you can't write
this line honestly, the entry isn't worth keeping.

## Visual characteristics

What makes it recognisable — proportions, weights, spacing, motion character, the specific
detail that carries it.

## Psychological effect

What it does to perception or behaviour: reduces perceived wait, increases trust, makes a
dense screen feel calm, makes an irreversible action feel safe.

## Best used when

The conditions that make this the right call — audience, frequency of use, data shape,
platform.

## Anti-patterns

Where this becomes wrong, and what it degrades into when applied without judgement. This
section is what stops the pattern from being over-applied later.

## Adaptations

How to transfer it to a different product category, or how to push it somewhere new. The
transfer is what makes it a pattern rather than a screenshot.
