Colour does one job (judged vs automatic); identity comes from labels and 2px gaps, so the 6 segments never need 6 hues. On phones the bar labels become a 2-column key with the number carried in each entry (the label row cannot wrap at 375). Measured 0 contrast fails both themes at 1440 and 375.
